🗣️ Dictpert: Czech Dialectology AI Assistant

What it is: An LLM chat assistant that answers questions about Czech dialects and regional vocabulary by consulting six dictionary and corpus sources — grounding every claim in retrieved data rather than the model's own (unreliable) training knowledge of dialectology.
Status: Live, actively developed since June 2026

🎯 Core problem: general-purpose LLMs hallucinate dialectal forms and geographic distributions with total confidence — Czech dialectology is too niche and regionally fragmented to be reliably in their training data. Dictpert instead searches real dictionary databases per query and cites exactly what it found.

How it works

Three answer modes, selectable per conversation:

  • Přímá odpověď (Direct) — a single streaming LLM call, no retrieval. Fast, but relies entirely on the model's own knowledge.
  • Se slovníky (Dictionary-augmented) — the model calls lookup/ftsearch/vsearch tools against the real dictionary databases across up to several rounds, then a post-loop pass verifies every citation traces back to an actual retrieved result. Optional Plan and Rethink modes let the model state a search strategy upfront or ask for more search when its own draft answer looks thin.
  • Vektorový RAG — semantic search pre-fetches relevant entries in one pass, for a faster single-call answer over the whole indexed corpus.

Six sources are searchable: ČJA (Český jazykový atlas — geographic distribution of dialect forms), SNČJ (Slovník nářečí českého jazyka — alphabetical dialect dictionary), SPJMS/SPJČ (toponym dictionaries for Moravia/Silesia and Bohemia), PSJČ (historical standard Czech), and ASSC (contemporary standard Czech, for etymology and normative context).
